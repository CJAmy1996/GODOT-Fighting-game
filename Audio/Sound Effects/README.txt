SE Collection — separated sound effects

Source: 54. SE Collection.mp3
Output: 171 individual MP3 files, 44.1 kHz stereo, 320 kbps.

Segmentation method:
- Quiet regions detected below -45 dB.
- A quiet gap had to last at least 350 ms to count as a boundary between effects.
- This threshold was selected after inspecting the silence-gap distribution: rapid internal pulses occur below ~250 ms, while deliberate between-effect gaps begin around ~480 ms.
- Rapid multi-pulse/compound effects are intentionally kept together rather than being chopped into tiny components.
- Each clip includes approximately 30 ms of pre-roll and 80 ms of tail room inside the surrounding silence when available, protecting attack transients and reverb tails.
- No normalization, noise reduction, EQ, compression, or fades were applied; the source's relative loudness is preserved.

See manifest.csv for exact source and clip timestamps.
